## Review Notes

SaldoPilot Pro is a non-consumable, one-time purchase. It unlocks the Budgets tab, where users can create monthly budgets by category and compare expected, spent, and remaining amounts.

No account or demo credentials are required.

Review steps:

1. Open Settings.
2. Tap SaldoPilot Pro.
3. Confirm that the localized price and Buy Pro button are displayed.
4. Complete the purchase using the App Review sandbox account.
5. Open the Budgets tab and confirm that category budgets are available.
6. If needed, use Restore Purchases on the SaldoPilot Pro screen.

The app uses StoreKit 2 and verifies transactions before unlocking Pro. The product identifier is `com.terjemoe.SaldoPilot.pro`.
