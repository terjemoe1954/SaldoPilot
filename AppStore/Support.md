# SaldoPilot Support

For help with SaldoPilot, use the GitHub issues page:

https://github.com/terjemoe1954/SaldoPilot/issues

When reporting a problem, include:

- Device model
- iOS or iPadOS version
- SaldoPilot app version and build number
- App language
- What you expected to happen
- What actually happened
- Steps to reproduce the issue

Useful test information:

- SaldoPilot does not connect to bank accounts.
- Payments are registered manually after you pay in your bank.
- Salary and other income are registered manually after the money is received.
- iCloud sync depends on the user's Apple ID and iCloud settings.
- Notifications require permission in iOS Settings > Notifications.

Do not include private financial details in public issues. Use general examples or anonymized amounts when possible.
