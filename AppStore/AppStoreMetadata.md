# SaldoPilot App Store Metadata Draft

## App Name

SaldoPilot

## Subtitle

Income, bills, and balance overview

## Promotional Text

Keep track of income, expenses, due dates, recurring payments, categories, reminders, and simple financial insights in one calm overview.

## Description

SaldoPilot helps you understand your everyday finances without turning them into a spreadsheet.

Track income and expenses, register due dates, mark payments as paid or received, organize transactions by category, and see what needs attention. The overview shows income, expenses, net balance, outstanding income, overdue items, and upcoming due dates.

SaldoPilot also supports recurring transactions, local reminders, statistics, JSON/CSV import and export, and simple local insights that summarize patterns in your registered transactions.

Key features:

- Income and expense tracking
- Due dates and paid/received status
- Recurring transactions
- Fixed translated categories
- Period overview and statistics
- Filters for date, status, type, category, and amount
- Local notifications for due dates and pending income
- JSON and CSV backup/export
- Import from old app exports
- Local financial insights
- Optional private iCloud sync through Apple

SaldoPilot is not a bank and does not provide financial advice. Insights are informational and based only on the data you enter.

## Keywords

budget, bills, income, expenses, finance, due dates, recurring payments, personal finance, backup, iCloud

## Review Notes Draft

The app stores user-entered financial transactions with SwiftData. iCloud/CloudKit private database sync may be used for the user's own data. AI-style insights currently run locally and do not send transaction data to an external AI service.
