# SaldoPilot Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-14

SaldoPilot is a personal finance app for tracking income, expenses, due dates, categories, recurring transactions, reminders, backups, and local financial summaries.

## Data Stored by the App

SaldoPilot stores the financial data you enter, including transaction titles, amounts, dates, status, categories, recurrence rules, notes, settings, language choice, and notification preferences.

SaldoPilot does not require a SaldoPilot account.

The app may use Apple's private iCloud/CloudKit sync if enabled for the app and your Apple ID. iCloud data is handled by Apple under your iCloud account.

## Data Collection and Tracking

SaldoPilot does not sell your data.

SaldoPilot does not use third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.

SaldoPilot does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

SaldoPilot does not connect to bank accounts and does not process payments.

## AI Features

AI-style summaries and questions in the current app version are generated locally from your transactions. Transaction data is not sent to an external AI service in the current app version.

AI suggestions are informational only. They do not automatically create, change, delete, mark, submit, or pay transactions.

The app includes a setting for external AI consent for possible future features. This setting does not send data today. If a future version adds external AI processing, the privacy policy should be updated and the app should ask for clear consent before sending transaction data outside the device/iCloud environment.

## Notifications

SaldoPilot can schedule local notifications for due dates and pending income reminders. Notification content is generated on device from your transaction data.

## Import, Export, and Reports

SaldoPilot can import and export files when you explicitly choose those actions. A JSON backup can contain transactions, budgets, relevant app settings, notes, and recurrence rules. A CSV export contains transaction data for use in spreadsheet apps.

SaldoPilot can also create PDF reports containing selected transaction data and financial totals. JSON, CSV, and PDF files are created locally. The app opens Apple's system share or print interface, and you choose whether and where to save, share, or print a file.

Exported files may contain sensitive financial data, so share and store them carefully. Purchase status and consent for possible future external AI processing are not included in backups.
