# SaldoPilot Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-14

SaldoPilot is a personal finance app for tracking income, expenses, due dates, categories, recurring transactions, reminders, backups, and local financial insights.

## Data Stored by the App

SaldoPilot stores the financial data you enter, including transaction titles, amounts, dates, status, categories, recurrence rules, notes, settings, and notification preferences.

The app may use Apple's private iCloud/CloudKit sync if enabled for the app and your Apple ID. iCloud data is handled by Apple under your iCloud account.

## Data Collection

SaldoPilot does not require a SaldoPilot account.

SaldoPilot does not sell your data.

SaldoPilot does not use third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.

SaldoPilot does not send transaction data to an external AI service in the current app version.

## AI Features

Current AI-style insights are generated locally from your transactions. Suggestions are advisory only and do not automatically create, change, delete, mark, or submit transactions.

If a future version adds external AI processing, the app should ask for consent before sending transaction data outside the device/iCloud environment.

## Notifications

SaldoPilot can schedule local notifications for due dates and pending income reminders. Notification content is generated on device from your transaction data.

## Import and Export

SaldoPilot can import and export JSON or CSV files when you explicitly choose those actions. Exported files may contain financial data, so share and store them carefully.
